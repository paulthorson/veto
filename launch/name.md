# Product name candidates

Criteria: sayable in one breath, memorable, hints at the honest/gatekeeper
angle ("the job bot that says no"). Avoiding famous products and crowded
hiring-tech names.

## The five

1. **Veto** — Names the actual mechanism: the constitutional veto screen that
   blocks dishonest or unqualified applications. One syllable, unmistakable,
   and the contrarian pitch ("the job bot that says no") in a single word.

2. **Merit** — Reframes the refusal positively: it applies only where you
   have merit. Warm where Veto is cold; says what it *does*, not what it
   blocks.

3. **Vett** — Short for "vetted." Every application is vetted before it goes
   out. Punchy and brandable. Caution: the hiring space already has several
   "Vetted" products, so this one risks confusion — would need a check.

4. **Doorman** — The doorman decides who gets in — including turning *you*
   away when you're not qualified. Has personality; people remember it.
   Minor collision: an unrelated delivery app used the name years ago.

5. **Goodfaith** — It applies in good faith: real experience, honest letters,
   a measured pace. Descriptive and ownable as one word. Slightly softer and
   longer than the others.

## Recommendation: Veto

Veto wins because it's the shortest, the most distinctive, and the only one
that names the differentiator itself rather than gesturing at it. "Veto, the
job bot that says no" is a complete pitch in eight words, and every demo —
especially the refusal moment — reinforces the name instead of explaining it.

Before committing: do a quick trademark/domain sanity check (no famous
product carries the name in dev tools, but verify), and grab the GitHub org
and social handles early. Runner-up if Veto is encumbered: **Merit**.
