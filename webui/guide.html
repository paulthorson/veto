$ tailscale-phone-setup

Get the Veto dashboard on your iPhone through Tailscale: encrypted end-to-end, works from any network. Tailscale is optional — Home Wi-Fi mode (same network, no extra software) is covered in docs/phone-access.md. Tick each step as you finish it.

1

Install Tailscale on your computer

Download it from tailscale.com/download, pick your OS, and install. Then sign in with your account.

2

Install Tailscale on your iPhone

Get the Tailscale app from the App Store and sign in with the same account as the computer. Flip the connection toggle on in the app.

3

Bring the computer onto your tailnet

In a terminal on the computer:

tailscale up

Then grab the computer's tailnet IPv4 address:

tailscale ip -4

You’ll see something like 100.64.0.7 — that’s your computer’s address on the encrypted mesh.

4

Start the dashboard on the tailnet IP

In the project directory:

python3 cli.py serve --host <tailnet-ip>

The server terminal prints an auth token at startup — keep it handy; it’s your login for the phone.

5

Open the dashboard on your iPhone

Make sure the iPhone’s Tailscale app is connected, then open Safari and go to:

http://<tailnet-ip>:8765

Enter the auth token from step 4 on the sign-in screen. You’re in — encrypted end-to-end, and it follows you off-network.

Lost the token? Regenerate it with python3 cli.py serve --regenerate-token, or delete ~/.veto_webui_token and restart the server — a fresh token is issued and the old one stops working.
